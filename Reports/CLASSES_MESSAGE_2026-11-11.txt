📢 *RK Classes — Software Update (11 Nov 2026)*

Aaj Web aur Mobile App dono me ye naye kaam add hue hain 👇

💰 *Finance & Fees*
• Finance me ab class + batch + medium wise (jaise 6th Morning Hindi / English) alag dikhta hai
• Total fees ab discount ke baad ka dikhega (jitna paisa milna hai) — discount sirf info ke liye
• App me bhi Additional Charges add / change kar sakte hain
• Ek button se sab due students ke parents ko fee reminder

🧾 *Expenses & Teacher Payment*
• Teacher ki paid salary ab Expenses me bhi judti hai
• Pichla mahina bhi dekh sakte hain + category / date / search filter
• Teacher payment ko Edit aur Delete kar sakte hain
• Payment Mode (Cash, Online, Cheque…) ab Masters se manage hota hai
• Teacher Payment Summary ko Excel me export kar sakte hain
• Faculty me ab Fixed salary / Hourly / Topic type set hota hai

👨‍🎓 *Students*
• Discontinued Students — jo student chhod gaya ya nikala gaya, uska reason, date aur paid / pending ke saath record (wapas lene ka option bhi)
• Delete ab 2 step: Active → Inactive → Deleted. Ek saath kai students select karke delete kar sakte hain
• Inactive students ab kisi count me nahi ginte
• Marks aur Attendance me ab sirf chuni hui academic year ke students aate hain

📅 *Attendance*
• Kisi batch ka holiday mark kar sakte hain — parents ko "Aaj class nahi hai" notification jata hai (cancel bhi ho sakta hai)
• Absent list class → batch → medium wise, aur jis parent ko message chala gaya wo green dikhta hai
• Pichli date ki attendance bhi mark / update ho sakti hai

🎂 *Birthday*
• Student ke birthday par parent / student login me "Happy Birthday from RK Classes" wish dikhti hai (text Masters se edit hota hai)
• Admin ko aaj ke birthdays dikhte hain

🔐 *Access control*
• Student / Parent login me kaun sa section dikhana hai (Lectures, Fees, Marks…) ab Admin on/off kar sakta hai
• Har role ke default rights set kar sakte hain

⚙️ *Experience*
• Har jagah loading dikhti hai (page, save, delete) — double click ka problem nahi
• Web aur App dono me sab kuch barabar
